07 — Sources & Methodology

The live map is refreshed hourly from official IMD statewise rainfall observations, with Timeanddate India Weather used as a secondary location-level signal when reachable. IMD Warning GIS is the official warning reference. StatisticsTimes, NITI Aayog/ICED, PIB and the research documents provide demographic, hydrology, flood-context or methodology inputs rather than live rainfall observations.

Model scope: This dashboard predicts a rainfall-driven flood-risk indicator, not inundation. It uses only precipitation information from IMD and Timeanddate for the risk calculation. It does not claim that a rainfall category is an official flood warning. Historical days remain blank when a verified historical observation is not available through the live source feed. Future days use the official IMD 7-day subdivision rainfall forecast; forecast station coverage is shown directly and is not converted into invented rainfall amounts.
