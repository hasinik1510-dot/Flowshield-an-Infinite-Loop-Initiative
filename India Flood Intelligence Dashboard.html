Risk method: the map uses only rainfall/precipitation information from IMD Mausam and Timeanddate. IMD rainfall thresholds are used to translate the available precipitation signal into a rainfall-driven flood-risk indicator. The population figure uses StatisticsTimes' 2026 state/UT population projections; the affected figure is a transparent rainfall-exposure proxy calculated as population × (24h-equivalent rainfall ÷ 204.5 mm), capped at 100%. It is not a count of people actually flooded or evacuated. The IWA Publishing study is used only as a methodological guide: its MCDA framework identifies rainfall as the highest-weight factor, but this dashboard does not import its elevation, slope, drainage-density, river-distance, road-distance or land-use datasets. NITI Aayog is used as contextual guidance on India's flood exposure, not as a source of current state rainfall.

Model scope: This dashboard predicts a rainfall-driven flood-risk indicator, not inundation. It uses only precipitation information from IMD and Timeanddate for the risk calculation. It does not claim that a rainfall category is an official flood warning. Historical days are reconstructed from the available snapshot and future days are modeled from that snapshot; they are not retrieved historical/live feeds.
